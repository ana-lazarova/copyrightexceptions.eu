title: "Section 25 of the Copyright Law"
date: 2022

exceptions:
- dsm4
jurisdictions:
- CY
score: 2
description: "This exception allows for the reproduction and extraction of legally accessible works and other material for the purposes of text and data mining, unless the use has been expressly limited by the rightholders in an appropriate manner." 
beneficiaries:
- any user
purposes: 
- text and data mining
usage:
- reproduction (of 'intellectual property', incl. computer programmes, and related rights, other than press publications)
- making available to the public (of 'intellectual property' and related rights, other than press publications)
- reproduction (in whole or in part) (of copyrighted databases)
- extraction and re-use (of sui generis right databases)
subjectmatter:
- works (incl. computer programmes)
- databases
- phonograms
- film fixations
- broadcasts
- publications of previously unpublished works
- performances
- press publications (unclear)
compensation:
- no compensation required
attribution: 
- no attribution required
otherConditions: 
- lawful access
- "storage and reuse: reproductions and exports made in accordance with the exception may be retained for as long as necessary for text and data mining purposes"
- the exception applies, provided that the use of works and other subject matter has not been expressly limited by the rightholders in an appropriate manner, such as by machine-readable means in the case of content made publicly available online
remarks: "Section 25(4) explicitly states that the exception does not affect the application of the provisions of §24 (TDM for research purposes).<br /><br />In terms of subject matter, the approach to the implementation of the CDSM Directive differs from the referencing scheme of the pre-existing E&Ls system, where there is one main exception applying to 'intellectual property' (meaning works, incl. computer programmes, but also sui generis right databases, phonograms, broadcasts and publications of previously unpublished works) and a reference to related rights (meaning press publications and performances). Here, the provision of §25 covers 'works and other material'. In addition, according to §44(3), the provisions of §§ 24 to 43 apply to all works and other subject matter protected by national copyright law on or after the date of entry into force of the provisions of this Law. At the same time, §25 explicitly refers to different types of usage for different subject matter. In that, it does not refer to any use of press publications. The legal uncertainty surrounding whether press publications fall within the scope of the exception warrants a lower score.<br /><br/>The exception does not appear to be protected against circumvention via TPMs. Since many pre-existing exceptions are safeguarded against TPMs under §14B(3) and (4), this is most likely an omission on the part of the legislator during the implementation of Article 7(2) of the CDSM Directive. This omission, however, also warrants a lower score for the exception."
