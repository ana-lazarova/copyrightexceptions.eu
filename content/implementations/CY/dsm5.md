exceptions:
- dsm5
jurisdictions:
- CY
score: 1
description: "This exception allows for the digital use of works and other subject matter for the sole purpose of illustration in teaching, to the extent justified by the non-commercial purpose pursued, provided that such use is carried out (i) under the responsibility of an educational institution on its premises or in other places or through a secure electronic environment to which only pupils or students and the educational staff of the educational institution have access and (ii) the rightsholders receive fair remuneration. An excerpt from a work used may not exceed five percent of the entire work, excluding works of poetry, unless otherwise provided in a relevant agreement. In addition, the exception does not apply whenever appropriate licenses which respond to the needs and specificities of educational institutions are readily available on the market." 
beneficiaries:
- any user (under the responsibility of an educational institution on its premises or in other places or through a secure electronic environment)
purposes: 
- illustration for teaching
- non-commercial
usage:
- reproduction (of 'intellectual property' and related rights, other than press publications)
- making available to the public (of 'intellectual property' and related rights, other than press publications)
- reproduction (in whole or in part) (of copyrighted databases)
- extraction and re-use (of sui generis right databases)
subjectmatter:
- works (incl. computer programmes)
- databases
- phonograms
- film fixations
- broadcasts
- publications of previously unpublished works
- performances
- press publications (unclear)
compensation:
- rightsholders must receive fair remuneration
attribution: 
- use must be accompanied by a reference to the source including the name of the author, unless impossible
otherConditions: 
- use must be effected to the extent justified by the non-commercial purpose pursued
- use must be effected under the responsibility of an educational institution on its premises or in other places or through a secure electronic environment to which only pupils or students and the educational staff of the educational institution have access
- use must not exceed five percent of entire works, excluding works of poetry, unless otherwise provided in a relevant agreement
- the exception does not apply whenever appropriate licenses which respond to the needs and specificities of educational institutions are readily available on the market
- use through a secure electronic environment shall be deemed to be carried out exclusively within the territory in which the educational organization operates
remarks: "The Cypriot implementation of Art. 5 of the CDSM Directive is among the most restrictive in the EU. The exception is remunerated, and its application is conditioned on the availability of licences. There is also a restriction on the volume of the work to be used (up to 5%).<br /><br/>In terms of subject matter, the approach to the implementation of the CDSM Directive differs from the referencing scheme of the pre-existing E&Ls system, where there is one main exception applying to 'intellectual property' (meaning works, incl. computer programmes, but also sui generis right databases, phonograms, broadcasts and publications of previously unpublished works) and a reference to related rights (meaning press publications and performances). Here, the provision of §26 covers 'works and other subject matter'. In addition, according to §44(3), the provisions of §§ 24 to 43 apply to all works and other subject matter protected by national copyright law on or after the date of entry into force of the provisions of this Law. At the same time, §26 explicitly refers to different types of usage for different subject matter. In that, it does not refer to any use of press publications. The legal uncertainty as to whether press publications fall within the scope of the exception is another argument for a low score.<br /><br/>The exception is safeguarded against contractual override (§28(1)). However, it does not appear to be protected against circumvention via TPMs. Since many pre-existing exceptions are safeguarded against TPMs under §14B(3) and (4), this is most likely an omission on the part of the legislator during the implementation of Article 7(2) of the CDSM Directive. This omission, however, also warrants a lower score for the exception."
