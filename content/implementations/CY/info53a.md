title: "Section 7(2)(r) of the Copyright Law"
date: 2022
draft: false
weight: 50
exceptions:
- info53a
jurisdictions:
- CY
score: 1
description: "This exception allows for the use for the sole purpose of illustration in teaching or scientific research, if justified by the non-commercial purpose pursued, provided that rightholders receive fair compensation. An excerpt from a work used for the purposes of this exception may not exceed five percent (5%) of the entire work, excluding works of poetry, unless otherwise provided in a relevant agreement. In addition, the exception does not apply to the extent that appropriate licenses, which meet the needs and specificities of educational institutions, are readily available on the market." 
beneficiaries:
- any user
purposes: 
- illustration in teaching
- scientific research
- non-commercial
usage:
- any use
- reproduction and making available to the public (of 'intellectual property' and related rights, other than press publications - for online uses)
- extraction and re-use (of sui generis right databases - for online uses)
subjectmatter:
- works
- databases
- phonograms
- film fixations
- broadcasts
- publications of previously unpublished works
- performances
- press publications (unclear for online uses)
compensation:
- rightholders must receive fair compensation 
attribution: 
-  the source, including the name of the author, must be indicated, unless impossible
otherConditions: 
- the application of the exception is subject to the provisions of §§ 26 and 28 regarding the online use of works, meaning that no online use may occur outside the scope of digital and cross-border teaching
remarks: "The Cypriot implementation of art. 5(3)(a) of the InfoSoc Directive is in line with the one of art. 5 of the CDSM Directive, in that it is among the most restrictive in the EU. The exception is remunerated, its application is conditioned on the availability of licences, and there is also a restriction on the volume of the work to be used (up to 5%).<br /><br/>In addition, the exception does not cover online use, for which the provision of §7(2)(r) refers to §26 (digital and cross-border teaching). Thus, online use is subject to the conditions of §26, making it unclear whether those conditions also apply to uses for research purposes. Furthermore, it is unclear whether §26 extends to press publications."
